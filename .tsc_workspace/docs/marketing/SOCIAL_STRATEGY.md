# Social Media Strategy

## Tone of Voice
- **Indie:** Authentic, slightly messy, passionate.
- **Tone:** "Just added physics... broke everything lol" (Relatable).
- **Goal:** Build in public. Show progress, not perfection.

## Platforms
- **Twitter/X:** Gifs + Short updates.
- **Discord:** Detailed changelogs.
- **TikTok/Shorts:** "How it started vs How it's going".

## Common Hashtags
#gamedev #indiedev #unity3d #madewithunity #solodev
